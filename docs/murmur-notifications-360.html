Remote push is switched off at the build level

apps/mobile/plugins/withoutRemotePush.js deletes the aps‑environment entitlement from every iOS build. The app physically cannot receive a push from our servers.

Nothing is stored to push to

No push token table, no APNs key, no FCM key, no notification preferences, no send log. Zero rows of notification infrastructure in 34 migrations.

The hard part is already written

Seven insight types, a budget engine, a forecaster and a recurrence calendar already run as pure shared functions. They just have no way to reach a phone.

Finding 1

Murmur knows twelve things. It is permitted to say two of them.

Everything on the left is already computed, tested and shipped. All of it is trapped behind one condition: the user has to open the app first.

The three reminders we do send all ask the user for work. Not one of the five tells the user something useful about their own money.

Finding 2

Murmur stops talking on day 7, exactly when the user is leaving.

Reminders are local and scheduled from the device with a seven day horizon. Day 8 with the app unopened, the queue is empty and nothing can ever refill it.

Why it cannot be patched locally

Extending the horizon to 60 days just queues 60 stale copies of the same sentence. A message worth sending on day 21 depends on data that changes after the app closes.

Why the device cannot know

Bills generate server side at 06:00 daily. Budgets move as other devices sync. Billing state changes inside RevenueCat. None of it reaches a sleeping phone.

What closes it

One hourly server sweep that reads the same shared engines and pushes the single best thing, if there is one. Nothing else.

Finding 3

Two of four shipped platforms have never sent a notification.

The build

One sweep, one governor, six tables' worth of plumbing.

No new intelligence has to be written. The sweep calls the same shared functions both apps already call.

The discipline

More notifications is how you lose the user. Better ones is how you keep them.

Building 32 new messages without a hard ceiling would be worse than shipping none.

3xretention for users who got at least one push in 90 days
3.4xuninstall risk above 6 pushes a week
46%would switch push off at 2 to 5 a week if irrelevant
~50%iOS opt in for finance apps, so every send is scarce

Per family opt out

Turning off recaps must not turn off a billing failure. Android channels do this natively, iOS needs our own settings screen.

Quiet hours

Nothing between 22:00 and 08:00 local. profiles.timezone is already written on every launch.

Never repeat

The log makes each claim once. The same budget threshold cannot fire twice in a period.

Silence is a result

A quiet week is the sweep working, not failing. Nothing is sent to fill a slot.

The privacy objection, answered

The server already holds every amount, merchant and category, because that is what sync is. A server side sweep reads what is already there. Audio and raw_transcript stay on device, untouched. Nothing in the privacy policy has to change, and no third party SDK enters the app.

The App Store objection, answered

Guideline 4.5.4: push must be optional and marketing push needs consent. Every family above is either transactional or opt out, set from a real settings screen, with the permission prompt still preceded by an explanation. The pattern already passed review once in the onboarding habit step.
