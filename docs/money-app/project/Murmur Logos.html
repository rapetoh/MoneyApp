Logo Explorations

Murmur — six directions.

Six logo concepts, each rooted in a different metaphor: voice, breath, money, listening, quietness. Tested at app-icon scale (32 / 48 / 64 / 88px), in a wordmark, and on a dark hero. Pick a direction or two and I'll refine — multiple weights, alternate layouts, splash screen, and brand sheet.

The Listening Drop
01 · Voice → form
A speech bubble compressed into a soft droplet, with a single sound-pulse inside. The shape of a quiet word. Reads as both a chat bubble and a coin — two metaphors at once, neither shouted.

Quiet Aperture
02 · Letter → wave
A lowercase “m” drawn as two concentric arches — like a sound wave radiating outward, or a calm landscape. Letter and wave fused in one stroke. The most “monogram-y” option, but earns it.

Wordmark
Half Breath
03 · The exhale
A single half-disc — half full, half quiet. The negative space is the listening. Shape of a quiet exhale, or a coin half-shown. Most minimal of the set; ages best.

Coin & Wave
04 · Money meets voice
A coin shape with a soft wave engraved across it — the only direction that explicitly signals "money app" without going fintech. The most discoverable on a busy app drawer.

Cradled Mic
05 · Literal but warm
A microphone capsule cradled in a cupped hand-arc. The most explicit “voice app” read — useful for cold-traffic marketing pages where the metaphor needs to land in 0.5s.

Whisper Loop
06 · Continuous gesture
One unbroken stroke that loops back on itself — a closed feedback loop, drawn in a single breath. You log it, Murmur understands it, you see it. Animatable: it draws itself when the app opens.

Designer's pick · 01 — The Listening Drop

It is the only direction that says
“voice” and “money” in one shape,
without saying either out loud.

It survives the 32px home-screen test. It works in the lockscreen widget at 22px. It can be drawn one-handed on a napkin. It animates: the inner pulse breathes when the mic is live. And when the app feels quiet — at night, in a low-light theme — the droplet feels like it's holding the user's last thought.

What I'd ship

My pick: 01 · The Listening Drop. Strong concept, scales clean, ownable. Backup: 03 · Half Breath if you want the most timeless, geometric option.

What I'd kill

05 · Cradled Mic reads loudest at small sizes but pegs the brand to "voice app" forever — limits your room to evolve into other input methods.
