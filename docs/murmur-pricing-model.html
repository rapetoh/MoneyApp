How Murmur should make money

One model, chosen against the category's own numbers and against MonAi, the leader in voice expense tracking.

Give every new user 14 days of full Plus, no card. Then keep capture free forever and sell the thinking. Reverse trial: the reach of free, close to the conversion of a paywall.

Day 0 to 14Everyone is on Plus

Onboarding ends by naming the four things now switched on, and Today carries a quiet "Plus, 12d" chip. A silent trial converts nobody: the downgrade has to be visible before it happens.

Day 12 to 14One honest choice

"Keep Plus for $2.50 a month, or stay on Murmur Free." The paywall quotes what they said they came for. No countdown theatre.

AfterFree stays useful

Capture never stops working, so the habit survives and they keep telling people about it. The paid parts sleep until they subscribe.

Why this and not something else
5xPaywalls beat freemium on conversion (10.7% vs 2.1% at day 35). Ignoring that is leaving money on the table.
+10 to 40%Reverse trials lift conversion over plain freemium, because people decide after living with the paid product, not before.
$0Our cost of a free user: capture runs on a cheap model, fractions of a cent per expense. Only Ask Murmur is expensive, so Ask is what we cap and sell.
0Free tiers at MonAi. The category leader sells from day 8. We can be the one people can actually keep using, and still charge the same.
45.7%Conversion for trials of a fortnight or more, against 26.8% for the usual 3 to 7 days. Ours runs 14 days for that reason.
To put it live
Shipped: 14-day reverse trial, server-side; onboarding names what it unlocks; Today shows the days left; the paywall quotes the user's own goal.
Shipped: free limits enforced on the server, 3 Ask questions a month and Insights on the current month, with previews instead of locked screens.
You, in App Store Connect: monthly to $4.99, yearly stays $29.99, turn on Family Sharing, and lower the price tier for low-income countries.
I update the store listing and the site so the words match the model.
We read the numbers at 60 days. If trial to paid sits under 4%, we test MonAi's harder gate on new users only.
